Publishing Checklist

  1. Confirm supplier identity and shop details.
  2. Check product description, price and minimum order quantity.
  3. Review the product image and category.
  4. Choose whether the Verified badge should appear.
  5. Choose Free or Locked/Paid contact access and set the fee.
  6. Confirm the listing is ready before publishing.
  7. Keep supplier contact information private.
