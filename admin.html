Product Publishing Checklist

  1. Confirm the supplier and shop information.
  2. Confirm the product is accurately described.
  3. Upload a clear image of the actual product.
  4. Assign the correct product category.
  5. Check wholesale price, suggested price and MOQ.
  6. Choose the Verified badge setting.
  7. Choose Free or Locked/Paid contact access.
  8. Set the unlock fee where applicable.
  9. Publish only when the image and required details are ready.
