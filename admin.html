Product Publishing Checklist

  1. Confirm the supplier and shop information.
  2. Confirm the product is accurately described.
  3. Upload the actual product image to GitHub.
  4. Enter the correct image path.
  5. Assign the correct product category.
  6. Check wholesale price, suggested price and MOQ.
  7. Choose the Verified badge setting.
  8. Choose Free or Locked/Paid contact access.
  9. Set the unlock fee where applicable.
  10. Publish only when all required details are ready.
NiPlag backend: Administrator login and Firestore product records are connected. Product images are hosted in GitHub. Supplier contacts remain separately protected.
