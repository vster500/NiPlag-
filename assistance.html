This is a purchase request, not a confirmed order. NiPlag must first confirm stock, product specifications, prices, service charges and delivery arrangements. You will receive a quotation for approval before payment.

A few things to know

Can I request products from different shops?

Yes. Tell us the products you need. We will assess whether they can be sourced and combined into one order.

Will delivery be free?

Not necessarily. Transport, delivery and service charges depend on the order and destination. These will be discussed in your quotation.

Does NiPlag guarantee every purchase?

Assisted purchases are handled under NiPlag's agreed order terms. Before payment, we will explain the purchasing, inspection, delivery and issue-resolution arrangements applicable to your order.

When should I pay?

Wait for NiPlag to confirm the quotation and payment instructions. Do not send money based only on this request form.
